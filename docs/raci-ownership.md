# RACI & Ownership (compact)

This document provides a compact RACI (Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed) mapping for core project activities. Use this as a quick reference; expand into team-specific RACI tables when needed.

Legend: R = Responsible, A = Accountable, C = Consulted, I = Informed

| Activity | PM | PdM | Dev | QA | Delivery Lead | Eng Manager | DevOps | Release Manager | Security | Compliance | Support Owner | Data Analyst | Tech Writer |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Project kickoff & charter | A | R | C | I | C | I | I | I | I | I | I | C | I |
| Backlog prioritization | C | A | C | I | R | C | I | I | I | I | I | C | I |
| Estimation & capacity | R | C | R | I | A | C | I | I | I | I | I | I | I |
| Feature delivery (sprint) | I | C | R | R | A | C | C | I | I | I | I | I | I |
| Release readiness & sign-off | R | C | R | R | C | C | R | A | C | I | I | I | R |
| Security review & sign-off | I | C | C | I | I | C | C | C | A | C | I | I | I |
| Incident triage & remediation | I | I | R | C | A | C | R | I | C | I | R | I | I |
| Post-incident retrospective | R | I | R | R | R | C | C | I | C | I | R | C | I |
| Metrics & success validation | C | A | C | I | I | I | I | I | I | I | I | R | I |
| Documentation & runbooks | I | C | C | I | I | I | C | C | I | I | C | I | R |

Notes
- Use this compact RACI as a starting point. For complex projects produce a dedicated RACI matrix per milestone with named owners.
- RACI should be reviewed during planning and updated when team composition changes.
